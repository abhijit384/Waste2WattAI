## Factor admission and deterministic calculation

Each future active coefficient must carry source organization, title, public HTTPS URL, publication date, category, pathway, unit, lower/typical/upper values, assumptions, geographic applicability and version. A typical value may be null; bounds alone are not turned into a point estimate. Runtime admission rejects incomplete provenance, non-finite/boolean coefficients and reversed bounds. A source record is evidence, not proof of scientific applicability: activation requires review of its complete scope and baseline.

Energy requires a matching category, pathway and geography, a justified typical kWh/kg coefficient, and an explicit basis: theoretical potential, gross output, usable electricity, useful heat or net output. The calculation is user quantity kg × coefficient. Carbon uses a complete baseline-minus-pathway life-cycle difference in kgCO2e/kg and requires the exact named baseline. Recycling, electricity and landfill offsets are not added independently; duplicate scenario units are rejected to prevent double counting. Negative carbon benefit is allowed where a reviewed scenario is worse than its baseline.

Unit conversions are exact conventions: grams ÷ 1000, metric tonnes × 1000, US short tons × 907.18474. Ordinary “ton” is rejected as ambiguous. Unknown values return `Estimate unavailable — verified conversion factor required.` Every result distinguishes scenario estimates from realized electricity or measured emission reductions.

Tests use clearly labeled synthetic coefficients solely to exercise formulas, baseline/geography matching and invalid provenance; they never enter the production catalog. No sustainability accuracy or benefit claim is made.

## Recovery rules

The pure engine evaluates material compatibility, contamination, separation, hazards and facility uncertainty. Organics suggest composting with AD as an alternative after acceptance checks; suitable material classes suggest recycling with reuse assessment; textiles prioritize reuse. Electronics go to authorized specialist handling; other possible hazards prevent ordinary treatment recommendations. Mixed/other/low-confidence results require review and separation. Facilities are UNVERIFIED and recommendations are not actionable until local acceptance is checked.

WTE and landfill are recognized options with explicit eligibility constraints, but not selected from a photo alone. WTE requires verified residual feedstock, calorific value and permitted emission controls; landfill requires authorized residual disposal after recovery assessment. Material percentages, calorific values and local facility availability are never fabricated. No automatic energy-recovery preference overrides appropriate recycling.
